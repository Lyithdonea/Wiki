Keen is a small fishing village located near the [[gateway-canton|Gateway Canton]].

## Locations
### Tavern
Serves food and drink. Also provides lodging for visitors (cheap option compared with the Gateway Inn). Locals like Meden and Dilave Andala rent out permanent rooms.
### Maupo Lagua's House
Maupo Lagua is a Keptu-Quey who operates the ship travel service from Keen. He does not own the boat. He is employed by the Llavayn family of the Gateway Canton.
### Karzhu Ghurak's House
Karzhu is an orcish mercenary. Possible connection to the now defunct "Guild of Fighters" bandit faction.
### Elammus Theray's House
Elammus Theray is a simple fisherman.
### Dreugh-jigger's Rest
Dreugh-jiggers hunt and harvest dreugh. They're not popular with the [[ouag-tong|Ouag Tong]] due to moral differences in what constitutes responsible fishing.
